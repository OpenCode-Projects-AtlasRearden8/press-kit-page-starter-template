LONG BIO — Dylan Asdale (about 200 words)

Dylan Asdale spent years as a registered nurse, where he learned that the
best explanations are patient, honest, and free of jargon. When he started
learning AI, he noticed that most resources either talked down to beginners
or buried them in buzzwords. So he started writing the kind of guide he
wished existed.

His first book, The Massive AI Manual, walks complete beginners through
everyday AI in plain English: what the tools actually do, how to use them
safely, and where they genuinely help. He maintains a companion hub at
dylanasdale.github.io.

In October 2026 he founded Asdale Dynamics, LLC, an Indiana company focused
on beginner-friendly AI education. The company publishes books, guides,
practical tools, and open-source templates on the same three rules Dylan
learned in healthcare: explain clearly, respect the reader, and never
pretend to be something you are not.

Dylan lives in Indiana. When he is not writing, he is probably playing
Final Fantasy XIV, losing gracefully at chess, or organizing one more
paperwork folder.
