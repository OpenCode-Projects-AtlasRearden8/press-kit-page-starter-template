SAMPLE INTERVIEW Q&As — Dylan Asdale

Starter answers for podcasters and journalists. Use verbatim or as
springboards; Dylan is happy to go deeper on any of these.

Q: You went from nursing to writing about AI. How does that happen?
A: Nursing teaches you to explain things at 3 a.m. to someone who is scared
and tired, with zero room for jargon. When I started learning AI, I noticed
most guides did the opposite: they either talked down to beginners or hid
behind buzzwords. I wanted to write the guide I wished I had, so I did.

Q: What makes The Massive AI Manual different from other AI books?
A: Three things. It is in plain English. It is step by step, so you finish
each small step before the next one starts. And it never assumes you already
know the secret handshake. Beginners are not slow; they are just new. There
is a difference, and I write for that difference.

Q: Should beginners worry about privacy with AI tools?
A: A healthy yes. The manual covers practical habits: what not to paste into
a chatbot, how to run AI on your own computer when you want the data to stay
home, and how to check what a service is doing with your files. You do not
need to be paranoid. You need a short, sensible checklist, which the book
provides.

Q: What is Asdale Dynamics, LLC?
A: An Indiana company I formed in October 2026 to publish my books, guides,
and practical tools, including open-source templates. The focus is
beginner-friendly AI education, plus author and small-business helpers like
publishing, marketing, and bookkeeping tools. Open-source where it makes
sense, honest about limits always.

Q: You write about healthcare workers and AI. What is your stance?
A: Education only, never medical advice. I am a nurse, not a replacement for
your care team. AI can help a busy nurse write a shift-handoff study plan or
learn a new tool, but patient decisions belong with licensed professionals.
The book makes that line very clear.
